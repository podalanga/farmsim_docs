# Tutorials

The tutorials teach FarmSim step by step with one robot, AmphiBot (see
[Overview](../get-started/index.md#the-example-robot)). Follow them in
order: each one builds on the previous.

| # | Tutorial | Level | Time | You will |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | [Your first simulation](first-simulation.md) | Beginner | 15 min | Run AmphiBot, use the viewer, find the output files |
| 2 | [Understand the experiment files](experiment-files.md) | Beginner | 30 min | Read the four YAML files and change the robot, the arena and the gait |
| 3 | [Trace a simulation step](simulation-workflow.md) | Intermediate | 30 min | Follow the code from the YAML files to a MuJoCo step |
| 4 | [Write a custom controller](custom-controller.md) | Intermediate | 45 min | Write a travelling-wave controller that switches gait between land and water |
| 5 | [Swimming and buoyancy](swimming.md) | Intermediate | 30 min | Configure the water, buoyancy and drag models |
| 6 | [Record, load and plot data](record-and-plot.md) | Beginner | 30 min | Load the HDF5 log and plot joints, trajectories and forces |

Every tutorial starts with its goal, level, time and prerequisites, and
ends with a summary and the next steps.

Before you start, [install FarmSim](../get-started/installation.md).
